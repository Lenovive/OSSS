# Code of Conduct

## The point

OSSS is a small technical project. The goal of this document is simply that
anyone who wants to work on frame generation can do so here without being
harassed, belittled, or driven off — including people who are new, people who
are wrong, and people who disagree with the maintainers.

## Expected behaviour

- Be civil. Criticise code, measurements, and ideas; not people.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until you have
  real evidence otherwise.
- Accept that "I measured it and you are wrong" is a normal, welcome thing to
  hear, and say it about the work rather than the author.
- Let people be new. Everyone's first pull request to a Direct3D codebase is
  rough.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks.
- Insults or demeaning comments about anyone, including about their identity,
  background, or level of experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or wearing people down through repetition.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and any
other project space, and to conduct elsewhere that is directed at project
participants because of their participation here.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you see or experience a problem, report it privately to the maintainers by
opening a [GitHub security advisory][advisory] (which is private and works fine
for a conduct report), or by contacting the repository owner through their
GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially; you will not be asked to
confront the other person.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close threads, and block accounts, in
proportion to what happened and to whether it keeps happening. A first offence
usually gets a private word; a pattern does not.

Maintainers are not exempt from any of this. A maintainer who breaks these
rules should be reported the same way.

[advisory]: https://github.com/Lenovive/OSSS/security/advisories/new
